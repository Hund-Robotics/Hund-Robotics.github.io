3 Degrees of Freedom (3-DoF) Per Leg

Each limb is partitioned into three independent rotational degrees of freedom arranged to maximize workspace volume while concentrating actuator inertia near the chassis trunk.

JOINT 1 • ROLL AXIS ($q_1$)

Hip Abduction / Adduction

Permits lateral leg splay and active roll stabilization over uneven terrain, trenches, and lateral slope contours. Direct mounted to chassis rail.

Joint Motion: ±35° (±0.61 rad)
Torque Limit: 18 Nm Continuous
JOINT 2 • PITCH AXIS ($q_2$)

Hip Pitch (Thigh Flexion)

Drives forward/backward swing phase and vertical stance thrust. Houses the planetary gearbox and upper carbon-composite femur link.

Joint Motion: -45° to +90°
Link Length ($L_1$): 210 mm (Femur)
JOINT 3 • KNEE PITCH ($q_3$)

Knee Pitch (Tibia Extension)

Delivers ground strike damping, foot placement height adjustment, and vertical leap kinetics. Actuated via lightweight linkage or concentric belt.

Joint Motion: -140° to -30°
Link Length ($L_2$): 225 mm (Tibia)

// PERCEPTION ARCHITECTURE

Vision & Semi-Autonomous Guidance

Combining stereoscopic optical depth sensing with a high-framerate IMU to achieve reliable GPS-denied state estimation.

Stereo Depth & Camera Vision

Dual forward-facing global shutter sensors with IR projection compute 3D point cloud disparity maps in real time, detecting obstacles, stair rises, and drop-offs.

• Depth Range: 0.2m – 6.0m
• Optical FOV: 87° × 58°
• Output: PointCloud2 + Color Streams

Semi-Autonomous Navigation

Supports waypoint tracking with automatic terrain adaptation. A human operator provides high-level heading vectors, while onboard reactive controllers handle leg balance.

• Teleoperation: 2.4 GHz Low-Latency Bridge
• Fail-safe: Auto-stance squat on comms loss
• Pathing: Nav2 ROS Costmap2D integration

9-DoF IMU & Odometry Fusion

An Extended Kalman Filter (EKF) fuses motor encoder joint states with high-frequency accelerometer/gyro readings and optical feature tracking to prevent drift.

• Sensor Hub: BNO085 9-Axis System
• Update Loop: 500 Hz State Estimator
• Drift Rate: < 1.5% over 50 meters

Capstone Timeline & Milestones

Structured over two senior engineering semesters culminating in the annual College of Engineering Design Symposium.

PHASE 01 • COMPLETE

CAD & Kinematics Model

Full SolidWorks assembly, mass distribution balancing, 3-DoF leg workspace validation, and Gazebo/MuJoCo physics simulations.

PHASE 02 • IN PROGRESS

Fabrication & QDD Testing

CNC 7075 aluminum leg brackets, 3D printed carbon-nylon enclosures, and single-leg dynamometer force-torque validation.

PHASE 03 • UPCOMING

Vision SLAM & Trot Gait

Integrating stereo cameras, implementing reactive Raibert trot controllers, and closed-loop semi-autonomous obstacle avoidance.

PHASE 04 • CAPSTONE FINALE

Symposium & Field Trials

Live untethered multi-terrain demonstration, publishing open-hardware technical whitepaper, and graduation symposium defense.
